# Blackwood Manor: The Thirteenth Chime
## Authorial Truth, Timeline, & Clue Consistency Architecture

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Confidential Author-Facing Canon**: This document establishes the objective, unalterable ground truth for the mystery *"Blackwood Manor: The Thirteenth Chime"*. In accordance with fair-play detective fiction principles, every fact necessary to deduce the murderer, weapon, motive, and sequence of events is discoverable through gameplay. No solution relies on information withheld from the player.

---

## 1. The Central Truth: What Actually Happened

On the tempestuous night of **October 14, 1922**, Lady Eleanor Blackwood was poisoned to death inside her locked study at Blackwood Manor.

The murderer is **Lord Julian Blackwood**, Eleanor's profligate nephew. Driven by catastrophic gambling debts amounting to over £8,000 owed to Mayfair underworld syndicates, Julian traveled from London to the manor under cover of the storm to demand an emergency bailout. 

When Lady Eleanor revealed that she had executed a new will stripping Julian of every penny and leaving the entire Blackwood shipping estate in trust to her estranged daughter, **Victoria Blackwood**, Julian premeditated her assassination:
1. **Source of Weapon**: Julian broke into the estate conservatory potting shed, stealing an amber apothecary vial of agricultural arsenic trioxide from caretaker **Arthur Briggs**'s bench.
2. **Delivery Vehicle**: Julian laced Eleanor's nightly valerian cordial in the private pantry and carried the silver flask to her study on a silver service tray under the pretense of familial reconciliation.
3. **The Mechanical Tampering**: Julian used a precision jeweler's file to notch the escapement wheel of the foyer's astronomical grandfather clock. He calculated that the jarring thirteenth chime would mask Eleanor's dying cries and create confusion regarding the exact time of death.
4. **The Locked Room**: Lady Eleanor ingested the poisoned cordial at approximately 11:30 PM. Experiencing severe abdominal spasms and realizing she had been poisoned, she locked the heavy oak study door from the inside to protect herself from further violence, collapsing in her wingback chair beside her dictation phonograph as the clock struck its impossible thirteenth chime at 12:13 AM.
5. **The Concealed Proof**: Before departing the manor into the storm, Julian hid his private correspondence, forged codicil drafts, and alchemist poison acquisition ledger inside the ancestral vaulted crypt cellar behind barrel rack 12, locking the iron gate with the family's duplicate brass crypt key.

## 3. Dramatis Personae: Profiles & Hidden Motivations

### 1. Arthur Briggs — The Caretaker
- **Role & Persona**: Caretaker of Blackwood Manor for 35 years. Pragmatic, taciturn, observant of locks, weather, and mechanics. Disdains spiritualism but harbors deep reverence for the Blackwood lineage.
- **Hidden Secret**: Arthur discovered his potting shed padlock sheared at 10:00 PM and noticed the rat poison missing. Julian caught Arthur inspecting the broken shed and violently threatened to evict him and cancel his unvested pension if he uttered a word to the authorities.
- **Moral Conflict**: Guilt over his enforced silence vs. terror of destitution in his twilight years.
- **How to Break His Guard**: Present physical proof of the clock escapement tampering (`clue_thirteenth_chime`) or the shed arsenic vial (`clue_arsenic_vial`). Once Arthur realizes Julian committed premeditated murder, his duty to Eleanor overrides his fear of eviction.

### 2. Victoria Blackwood — The Estranged Daughter
- **Role & Persona**: Intellectual, wounded, defensive, yet fiercely proud. Educated in London, she returned after a bitter estrangement sparked by her mother's authoritarian control.
- **Hidden Secret**: Victoria witnessed Julian throwing the disinheriting will into the hearth. She burned her hands pulling the fragment out. She kept quiet initially because the will made her the sole beneficiary, fearing Scotland Yard would suspect her of matricide.
- **Moral Conflict**: Fear of being wrongly accused vs. love for her mother and desire to clear her family's name.
- **How to Win Her Trust**: Present the charred will fragment (`clue_burned_will`) and show restraint. Victoria will recognize the investigator's genuine pursuit of justice, confessing that Julian tore his crimson velvet coat and providing the brass crypt key to unlock the vaulted cellar.

### 3. Lady Eleanor Blackwood — The Restless Spirit (Apparition)
- **Role & Persona**: The spectral presence of the deceased matriarch, tethered to the Séance Parlor by psychic agony and an unquenchable thirst for justice.
- **Hidden Secret**: Retains fragmented sensory trauma from her dying moments: the burning taste of copper and bitter almonds, Julian's mocking farewell kiss, and his laughter echoing as the clock struck thirteen.
- **Moral Conflict**: Wracked by betrayal from her own bloodline; in danger of descending into a vengeful poltergeist if the investigation fails or makes wrongful accusations.
- **How to Commune**: Present the murder weapon (`clue_silver_flask`) and the clock escapement (`clue_thirteenth_chime`). This anchors her fragmented memories, allowing her to bestow her final blessing and identify Julian's onyx signet ring.

### 4. Lord Julian Blackwood — The Culprit (Unseen Suspect / External Target)
- **Role & Persona**: Aristocratic sociopath, gambler, and disgraced nephew. Currently sequestered in a nearby inn awaiting the solicitor's probate reading.
- **Deductive Profile**: Julian is the only individual with:
  1. **Motive**: Immediate financial insolvency and total disinheritance confirmed by the burned will.
  2. **Opportunity**: Present in the study prior to midnight, seen carrying the cordial tray, and possessing the clock cabinet key.
  3. **Means**: Stolen arsenic matching the flask residue and documented in his hidden crypt ledger.

## 6. Endings Matrix & Deterministic Prerequisites

### Ending 1: The Truth Revealed (True Restitution) — Rank S
- **Outcome**: The complete conspiracy is laid bare. Julian is arrested on the morning express by Scotland Yard constables. Victoria inherits the estate with cleared conscience; Arthur's tenure is secured; Lady Eleanor's spirit departs in radiant peace.
- **Exact Engine Prerequisites**:
  - `suspect_id` = `"char_julian"`
  - `weapon_id` = `"clue_silver_flask"` or `"clue_arsenic_vial"`
  - `motive_id` = `"clue_burned_will"`
  - Supporting clues verified: $\ge 3$ (must include `clue_crypt_journal`, `clue_thirteenth_chime`, `clue_arsenic_vial`)
  - Caretaker trust $\ge 50$
  - Daughter trust $\ge 55$
  - Player sanity $\ge 40$

### Ending 2: The Wrong Accusation / Inconclusive Inquest (Tragic Coverup) — Rank C
- **Outcome**: Julian is named as the culprit, but without the poison ledger from the crypt or key corroborating testimonies, the coroner rules the case circumstantial. Julian absconds to the Continent with the estate funds. Arthur is dismissed, Victoria is ruined, and Eleanor's ghost haunts the silent halls forever.
- **Exact Engine Prerequisites**:
  - `suspect_id` = `"char_julian"`
  - `weapon_id` = `"clue_silver_flask"`
  - `motive_id` = `"clue_burned_will"`
  - Incomplete supporting evidence ($< 3$ supporting clues or missing `clue_crypt_journal`)
  - Player sanity $\ge 20$

### Ending 3: The Silent House / Spectral Wrath — Rank F
- **Outcome**: An innocent party (Arthur Briggs or Victoria Blackwood) is accused, or the investigator succumbs to madness (Sanity reaches 0). The desecrated truth unleashes poltergeist fury. The ward candle detonates in black flame, shattering the windows and sealing the manor in eternal darkness.
- **Exact Engine Prerequisites**:
  - `suspect_id` $\ne$ `"char_julian"` (e.g. accused Caretaker or Daughter), OR
  - Player sanity $\le 0$.

## 7. Fair-Play Inquest Rules
1. Every clue possesses a distinct physical discovery location in the mansion.
2. Character testimonies corroborate physical evidence:
   - Arthur verifies the clock file marks and shed break-in.
   - Victoria identifies the crimson velvet coat and provides the crypt key.
   - Eleanor identifies the poison's sensory signature and Julian's presence.
3. No secret twin, supernatural impostor, or unmentioned character committed the deed. Julian's presence, debts, and movements are established from the opening moments.
